Ramakrishna Math Nagpur

Ramakrishna Math Nagpur is a branch centre of Ramakrishna Math and Ramakrishna Mission, Belur Math, Howrah, dedicated to the ideals of Sri Ramakrishna, Sri Sarada Devi, and Swami Vivekananda through spiritual, educational, cultural, and service activities.

Visit Official Website →

📚 Publications and Books

The Publication Department of Ramakrishna Math Nagpur publishes a variety of spiritual, philosophical, cultural, and educational books in Marathi and Hindi. The centre also publishes the Marathi spiritual and cultural magazine Jivan Vikas.

Online Book Store →

📖 Jivan Vikas Magazine

Jivan Vikas is a Marathi magazine published by Ramakrishna Math Nagpur. It contains articles and reflections on spirituality, philosophy, culture, values, and the teachings of Sri Ramakrishna, Sri Sarada Devi, and Swami Vivekananda.
